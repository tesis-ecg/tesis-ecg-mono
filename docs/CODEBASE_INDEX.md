**Core infra** (`back/app/core/`): `config.py` (typed environment/config validation), `security.py` + `jwt_codec.py` (stdlib HS256 and cryptography-backed RS256, fixed algorithms, iss/aud/jti/sessionVersion), `auth0_client.py` (ROPG, Management API, JWKS cache), `request_context.py` (validated Vercel client IP), `logging.py`.

**Dependencies** (`back/app/dependencies/`): `common_dependencies.py` yields an async session without implicit commit. `device_dependencies.py` authenticates the Holter itself (`X-Device-Serial` + bearer compared against `device.api_key_hash` with `hmac.compare_digest`); it shares nothing with the user auth path — the device has no session and no cookie. `auth_dependencies.py` accepts migration cookies (`holter_session_v2` then legacy `session`), validates sessionVersion and active identity, and returns an explicit `RoleScope`: `ADMIN_GLOBAL` or `DOCTOR` with mandatory doctor ID — it **rejects the `paciente` role**, which is what keeps the mobile account out of the portal. `patient_dependencies.py` is the mobile counterpart: `Authorization: Bearer` only (never a cookie), with its own JWT audience (`jwt_mobile_audience`) so a portal token cannot be replayed on `/mobile` or vice versa.

**ML** (`back/app/ml/`): `frame_header.py` holds the header parser and frame splitting with no numpy (the ACK path imports only this, so a cold start does not pay for the processing stack); `decompression.py` is **implemented** — the Rice frame decoder, a verbatim port of the firmware's normative decoder (`../Holter-ECG-System/tools/holter_frame_decoder.py`). **Two analysis passes coexist (not unified yet; doctor-facing HR/HRV numbers come from the Holter metrics pass):** (1) *Holter metrics* — `rpeak_detection.detect_r_peaks` (Pan-Tompkins), `st_analysis.py` (ST level per beat at R+100 ms), `beats.py` (persisted beat format `(sample_index, st_mv)`), `hrv.time_domain`/`frequency_domain` (Task Force definitions, `pnn50Percent` in %) and `holter_metrics.py` (pure aggregation: RR validity across timeline cuts/quality exclusions, pauses, HR min/max with evidence, hourly trend, RR histogram, ST episodes); its numpy-free contracts (`EXCLUSION_KINDS`, `BREAK_KINDS` — including the engine-side `frame_gap` —, `QUALITY_EXCLUSION_REASONS`, `HARDWARE_QUALITY_REASONS`, `TimelineRun`) live in `holter_contracts.py` so `studies_service` can import them at boot. There is **no beat classification**, so S/V/abnormal-beat metrics are `null`. (2) *Detection engine* (`pipeline.analyze_batch`, behind `settings.ml_enabled`) — `quality.py` (per-window SQI gate, including the NeuroKit-vs-firmware `FLAG_R_PEAK` agreement `bSQI` from `rpeak_detection.detect_rpeaks`/`beat_sqi`), `morphology.py` (per-patient template bank; unusual morphology becomes an `ANOMALY` finding), `arrhythmia.py` (rhythm rules on the R-R series of `hrv.build_rr`), `episodes.py`, `intervals.py` (experimental QT/QTc/R amplitude; behind `ml_interval_measurements_enabled` each block writes one research-only `ecg_interval_measurement` row that no API, report or finding reads, exported with `python -m app.scripts.export_interval_measurements`, which writes no wall-clock date and skips soft-deleted patients), `totals.py` (exact study-level accumulators; `pnn50` as a fraction) and `contracts.py` (dataclasses at the boundary). `core/workers.py` runs it off the event loop.

**ML: pausas por hueco quieto** (`quiet_gap.py`). `arrhythmia.detect_rhythm` solo mira R-R válidos, y una asistolia que deja una ventana de 10 s sin QRS sale `bad` (psqi/ksqi/bassqi o `flatline`): el motor no informaba pausa ni aviso. `pipeline.analyze_batch` pasa el ritmo por `quiet_gap.refine_pauses` con un `GapEvidence` (señal, flags, señal limpia, R de NeuroKit y del firmware, `QualityReport`, `flatline_mv`, contextos del bloque). Informa una pausa (`metadata.quietGap`; CRITICAL desde 3 s) entre dos R consecutivos que cortan el hueco cuando, medido contra los latidos **del propio paciente**:

- **Referencia y cotas.** Los latidos a ±60 s de los dos R (nunca su centro, que en una asistolia es el hueco): su población dominante (`REFERENCE_FLOOR`), al menos 5 (`MIN_REFERENCE_BEATS`) y, con el firmware arbitrando, 5 confirmados por él. Un **censo** exige que sean el ritmo del paciente (`CENSUS_MIN` de los R legibles de NeuroKit que miden ≥ `CENSUS_FLOOR`; los que no tienen T, `CENSUS_T_MIN`, son las P de un bloqueo AV completo y no cuentan). Un R acota si mide 0,6-3× la referencia, si el firmware lo confirmó (≥ 0,15×, sin tope) o si abre o cierra un **tren de escape** (`_train`: 3 R parejos a 50 lpm o menos, más grandes que la P del paciente; el detector del MCU no ve un escape ancho y chico). Una cota por debajo de 0,6× tiene que sobresalir de lo quieto que cierra y, si es **de transición** (la siguen latidos en ritmo que vuelven a crecer, `_Block.transitional`), no tener la forma de los latidos del paciente (`ATTENUATED_SHAPE`): es el colapso de amplitud que se recupera (MIT-BIH 116 y 208).
- **Interior quieto** (`[R1 + 0,5 s, R2 − 0,3 s]`, `QUIET_MAX` en banda ancha y en banda QRS). Las **P del paciente** (`P_WAVE_MARGIN` × la P que precede a sus latidos de referencia) no cuentan, y hasta 2 transitorios cortos no anulan el hueco: los que tienen pendiente de latido lo parten en tramos (`_Block.stretches`). Una extrasístole **temprana** (`_Block.early_beat`, 0,25-0,5 s después del primer R) es el latido desde el que corre la pausa.
- **Contacto sin cambios** (red y deriva ≤ 2,5× las de las ventanas vecinas) y ningún R del firmware adentro.
- **No falta señal** (`_Block.missing`): `LEAD_OFF`, saturación, `frame_gap`, `SQ_BAD` del firmware, ventanas `lead_off`/`saturated`/`flatline` y un **riel** (`rail_mask`: 1 s de señal cruda con p5-p95 por debajo de 0,6 × `ml_flatline_uv`, aunque no llene una ventana).

**Tramos abiertos** (`openStart` / `openEnd` en `metadata`, en lugar de `firstBeatRatio` / `lastBeatRatio`): donde falta un R se informa lo que se sabe. `openStart`: una asistolia de más de ~90 s (60 s de contexto + 30 s de contexto derecho) no cabe en la lectura de un bloque, y la informa el que lee el R que la cierra, desde el principio de su lectura. `openEnd`: quieto hasta el final de la lectura, desde 10 s (la asistolia en curso; el bloque que lee el R que la cierra la completa). Y los bordes de una ráfaga de ruido (`_Block.stretches`, desde 3 s): a los 8-10 s el paciente se desmaya o convulsiona y el artefacto anulaba la pausa entera. Lo que dos bloques informan de una misma asistolia se solapa y la persistencia lo empalma con un solo aviso (`episodes.open_edges` resuelve qué lado quedó abierto).

La misma medida depura las pausas que el motor ya daba (`refine_pauses`): descarta las que tienen adentro algo del tamaño de un latido **con la pendiente de un QRS** (`ENGINE_VETO`, `ENGINE_VETO_QRS`; 46 falsas en MIT-BIH) y las que cruzan un riel, y las que tienen una extrasístole temprana corren desde ella (o se descartan si dejan de ser pausa). `explained_by_pauses` evita pintar como `noise_burst` las ventanas que una pausa explica (siguen `bad` en calidad y totales). El gate no cambió.

**Límites conocidos** (lista completa en el docstring del módulo): un colapso de amplitud a ≤ 0,2× sin cambio de contacto, que el firmware no ve y con cotas creíbles, no se distingue de una asistolia —la regla elige avisar—; QRS normales de ≤ 0,2× de las extrasístoles que el firmware confirma solas se miden como P; una extrasístole de ≤ 0,2× que nadie confirma (o una muy ancha de 0,35×) deja una pausa N-N con ella adentro; una P de más de 0,4× el QRS no se cree y, con un bloqueo AV completo, las P no se miden; una línea de base de σ ≤ 6 µV llena ventanas `flatline` y no se infiere nada; de un síncope convulsivo a veces sale solo un lado, o nada. Tests: `back/tests/test_ml_asystole.py` (y `open_edges` en `test_ml_episodes.py`); medición: `tools/physionet` (`--pauses`, `--wander`, `--firmware-peaks`) y las guardas de ritmo de `tools/vest/evaluate.py`.

**ML: taquicardia y bradicardia a través de ventanas ilegibles** (`arrhythmia._bridged_runs`). Dos tramos del mismo ritmo separados **solo** por R-R inválidos se unen si el hueco no pasa de `MAX_INVALID_BRIDGE_SECONDS` (25 s: dos ventanas de calidad más el R-R de cada borde a 30 lpm); un R-R válido que no cumple el umbral sí corta. El mínimo de `ml_rhythm_min_seconds` se mide sobre lo **leído**: el hueco une pero no suma. Lo motivó el simulador de chalecos: el gate rechaza ventanas sueltas de un ritmo limpio (kSQI < 5 a 155 lpm con bSQI = 1,00; basSQI 0,89 a 40 lpm) y una taquicardia continua de 6 min salía como tres eventos y tres avisos. Sobre MIT-BIH el kSQI rechaza ventanas sueltas a toda frecuencia (9 % a 50-100 lpm) y no se cae con la frecuencia como en el ECG sintético, por eso se puenteó el ritmo y el gate no cambió. Tests: `test_ml_block_context.py` (`ilegible`, `bradicardia_partida`, `hueco_largo`).

**ML: morfología a la frecuencia del latido** (`morphology.py`, `hrv.expected_rr`). El score recorta la ventana comparada con el R-R **esperado** (`scoring_window`, mediana local; con el de la dominante si ella se aprendió más rápido) para que no entren la T del latido anterior ni la P siguiente, y `dissimilarity_to_dominant` recibe un `BeatRate` que compara contra la dominante a la frecuencia del latido (`Template.mean_expected_rr`; la repolarización se comprime o se estira, el QRS no se adapta). Un prematuro (`hrv.prematurity` < `hrv.ECTOPIC_PREMATURITY`) conserva el lado izquierdo entero —la T anterior sobre su P es lo que lo delata— y se compara sin adaptar. Sin eso una taquicardia sinusal de esfuerzo salía como episodios `ANOMALY` y como un foco recurrente de latidos normales. El encabezado `recurrent_morphology` exige además que la plantilla haya puntuado (`morphology.is_recurrent`, `ml_recurrent_cluster_min_anomalous_fraction` = 0,20; contadores `scored_count`/`anomalous_count` y marca `Template.reported` que no se deshace, `mark_reported`); agrupar episodios sigue usando solo el conteo (`TemplateBank.recurrent_ids`). **Cuando cambia la dominante** (`morphology.hand_over_dominance`, desde `analyze_batch`), la que la pierde juzga por su centroide los miembros que no pudieron puntuar (se plegaron mientras era la dominante) y la que la gana vuelve a contadores en cero y **retira su encabezado**: `PipelineResult.retracted_headers` lleva los `cluster_id` y `ml_persistence.persist_analysis` da de baja (soft delete) sus filas `cluster:<id>` de alcance `study`. Evita que una dupla o un bigeminismo de los primeros diez minutos deje en la base el encabezado de la forma **normal**. Los `cluster_id` y la representación guardada no cambian; el estado persistido del banco suma los contadores y el R-R esperado acumulado; un banco viejo carga con `reported` sin resolver (`bank_from_state`) y `pipeline._mark_reported` lo resuelve con la regla de antes. Test: `back/tests/test_ml_morphology_rate.py`.

**Migrations** (`back/alembic/versions/`): `4940f777d181_initial_schema.py` → `27e0b772f1dd_device_doctor_id.py` → `8c1f2a7d9b30_security_and_integrity.py` → `a1b70fd51903_ecg_ingest_pipeline.py` (ingest columns on `study`/`ecg_batch`, `DEVICE_API_KEY_ROTATED` audit type) → `b2c3d4e5f6a7_study_lifecycle_and_alerts.py` (`STUDY_COMPLETED`, `STUDY_CANCELLED`, `ALERT_ACKNOWLEDGED` audit types; no table changes) → `c3d4e5f6a7b8_patient_app.py` (restores the `paciente` value in `user_role`, adds `patient_report` and `push_token`, makes `alert.event_id` nullable and adds `alert.kind`) → `f6a7b8c9d0e1_wall_clock_timeline.py` (adds `study_timeline_segment`, `study.ecg_level_carry`, and the bridge time-sync columns on `ecg_batch`) → … → `d0e1f2a3b4c5_holter_beat_analysis.py` (`study.ecg_beat_chunks`, `study.beats_analyzed_samples`) → `32266feb49f2_ml_findings.py` (`ANOMALY` event type; `ecg_event.study_id`/`model_version`/`dedupe_key`/`validation_*`; `signal_quality_interval`; `ecg_interval_measurement` (FKs `ON DELETE CASCADE`); `study.ml_state` and the `ml_analyzed_samples` cursor, set to `samples_count` for existing studies; stale-tail indexes on `ecg_batch`). ## Cross-App Request Flows

**Auth/login**: browser uses same-origin `/api` → Vite/Vercel proxy → `POST /auth/login` → PostgreSQL HMAC rate limit → Auth0 ROPG → verified Auth0 RS256 token → pre-provisioned active DB identity only → internal versioned JWT. During migration login emits `holter_session_v2` (`HttpOnly`, `Secure` outside dev/test, `SameSite=Lax`, `Path=/api`) plus legacy `session`; logout increments sessionVersion and removes both.

**User provisioning**: login never auto-provisions. Admin-only `/users` owns the pending → active/error Auth0–DB workflow; deprecated `/auth/register` delegates to it. `app.scripts.reconcile_identities` repairs pending/error states. Inconsistent identities cannot authenticate.

**ECG viewing**: StudyDetail prefers `GET /studies/{id}/ecg/manifest` (**manifest v3**: versioned little-endian raw metadata + SHA-256 + presigned min-max level *chunks*, 10-minute expiry, normalized `annotations` carrying absolute `startEpochMs`/`endEpochMs`, and a `timeline` of contiguous recording runs). The viewer's X axis is real wall-clock time and gaps in the recording are drawn as gaps; see `docs/adr/005-linea-de-tiempo-de-pared.md`. `studies_repository.list_ecg_events` associates modern events through `ecg_batch.study_id` and falls back to legacy `metadata.studyId`; the service clips sample/time coordinates to the recording and exposes lower-case kind/category/severity. The client selects a pyramid level capped at 20,000 points, validates size/checksum, overlays annotations in uPlot/minimap, and drives the findings panel from the same payload. A 404 falls back to deprecated `/ecg` with no annotations. S3 downloads remain direct and private/presigned.

**ECG ingestion (device → study)**: the vest POSTs `N × 256 B` Rice-compressed frames as `application/octet-stream` to `/ingest/ecg-frames`. The service validates each frame (magic → version → CRC-32), resolves `serial → device.patient_id → open in-progress study` (creating one if needed, and never joining a study that already holds a legacy `ecg_s3_key` blob), archives the accepted contiguous run to S3 and answers `202` with a go-back-N ACK delta. A `BackgroundTasks` job then decodes to float32 mV, writes one segment plus one bucket-16 envelope per batch, **appends one chunk per coarse pyramid level** (O(batch), not O(study) — rebuilding every level on every batch is what produced `500`s under sustained ingest), places the batch on the study's **wall-clock timeline** (`modules/ingest/timeline.py`), runs `append_beat_analysis` in its own SAVEPOINT via `_guarded_beat_pass` (cursor `beats_analyzed_samples`, 30 s context, active-run tail waits, capped at 2 h per pass; signal no timeline run covers is skipped, like the engine's `_pending_blocks`; beat chunks in S3 under `studies/{id}/beats/`, compacted like the pyramid), and derives `ecg_event`/`alert` rows. After draining batches, `process_batch` runs one detection-engine pass (`append_ml_analysis`, in a SAVEPOINT via `_guarded_ml_pass`; both wrappers use `processing._guarded`, which logs and refreshes on failure but re-raises lock contention so the batch stays pending) over whole 300 s blocks of each timeline run behind the `ml_analyzed_samples` cursor; `process_study_task` loops those passes (committing between them) plus the beat pass, and `_finalize_closed_study` merges morphologies and force-compacts beats/pyramid once a closed study is fully analyzed. The lifespan `sweep_stale_tails_forever` flushes open runs that stopped growing (`ml_open_tail_flush_minutes`). Metrics are recomputed on demand from the beats (`studies_service._holter_metrics`) and frozen into the clinical report snapshot (`schemaVersion` 2, with `analysis.algorithmVersion`, today 3) together with evidence strips for HR min/max and the longest pause. Studies ingested before this need `python -m app.scripts.backfill_beat_analysis`; `backfill_timeline` resets `beats_analyzed_samples`/`ecg_beat_chunks` of every study whose run layout it changes, so a prefix the beat pass skipped is re-analyzed instead of counted as beat-less analyzed time. Levels are compacted into one object per level when the chunk count crosses a threshold, and again when the study closes. Close-time compaction (raw pyramid, filtered pyramid and, once `beats_analyzed_samples` reaches `samples_count`, the beat chunks in its own `_guarded` SAVEPOINT) runs inside `process_study_task` → `_finalize_closed_study`, **under the study row lock** taken by `_lock_study` until the commit, with the S3 I/O in `asyncio.to_thread` so it never blocks the event loop. `studies_service.compact_study_pyramid` is only used by tests (its `compact_study_pyramid_task` wrapper, inherited from main, is never scheduled). `/ingest` is exempt from the Origin check — it carries no cookie, so it has no CSRF surface, and the ESP32 co-processor sends no `Origin` header.

**Patient app session**: the app never uses a cookie. `POST /mobile/auth/login` takes an **email or a DNI** (a DNI is translated to the account's email before the Auth0 ROPG call, so the endpoint cannot be used to enumerate patients), and returns an access token (60 min) plus a refresh token (60 days) in the response body. Both carry `session_version`, so logout invalidates the pair with no refresh-token table. `/mobile` is exempt from the Origin check for the same reason `/ingest` is: no cookie, no CSRF surface.

**Patient log entry → ECG annotation** *(the subtle one)*: the vest uploads roughly hourly, so a patient can record a symptom long before the signal for that instant exists. `patient_report` therefore stores **wall-clock `occurred_at`**, and `studies_service._report_placements` decides where each entry sits at read time. A **spontaneous** entry is placed by `_report_offset_ms`: `offset > samples_count/sample_rate` means it is **omitted** from the manifest — deliberately not clipped like `_event_offsets_ms` does, because clipping would pin every recent report to the right edge of the trace — and it migrates into a band on its own once the covering batch lands. An entry that **answers an alert** (`alert.event_id` → a drawn finding) is instead anchored to the **midpoint of that finding's band** and carries `linkedAnnotationId` + `description` (symptoms): the patient replies when the notification arrives, minutes after the finding, so by wall clock the answer would sit in ungrabbed signal and never appear — and loose on a 24 h trace there would be no way to tell which alert it answers. `GET /studies/{id}/patient-reports` reuses the same placement (so `visibleInChart` and `offsetMs` always match the chart) and adds `alertKind`. Front side: `annotationMeta.buildAnnotationLinks` resolves the pair in both directions, labels the chart marker `Respuesta: <finding>`, highlights both when either is selected, and `compareAnnotationsForPainting` draws instants after bands so the answer is not buried under the finding it sits inside. In `ECGFindingsPanel` the answer is **not** a top-level row (it would inflate the "avisos detectados" count with something nothing detected): it renders nested inside its finding's card, indented behind a vertical rule, still its own button so it can jump to its marker.

**Push**: `core/push.py` exposes a `PushSender` protocol with `ExpoPushSender` (Expo Push Service) and `NoopPushSender` (used whenever `expo_push_enabled` is false — tests and CI never reach the network). Three triggers, all dispatched through `BackgroundTasks`: `POST /ingest/device-status` (vest reports sustained bad signal; the debounce is **per episode** — `vest_status_debounce_minutes` only absorbs a repeat while `device.placement_ok` is already false, so a `signal_recovered` re-arms the notification), `ingest/processing._persist_events` (one push per processed batch, for the most severe HIGH/CRITICAL alert), and admin/dev-only `POST /studies/{id}/simulate-anomaly` (the manual stand-in for the ML pipeline: writes an `ecg_event` anchored inside already-ingested signal plus its `alert`, so the push carries an `occurredAt` that falls inside the recording and the patient's answer lands on the chart instead of waiting for the next batch). All three converge on `patient_app_service.schedule_alert_push` / `notifications_service.notify_patient_task`. Tokens that Expo answers `DeviceNotRegistered` are soft-deleted in the same call. En mobile, `NotificationsBridge` espera la restauración de sesión, lleva anomalías al modal de reporte, mala colocación a Dispositivo y payloads incompletos al centro `/notifications`; un push —recibido en foreground o tocado desde el fondo— invalida las cachés de avisos **y** de dispositivo. `registerForPushNotifications` no corta por `Device.isDevice`: es `false` también en el emulador de Android, que es donde las push se prueban gratis.

**Holter assignment**: `front` devices/patient UI → `POST /devices/{id}/assign|unassign|reassign` → `devices_service` maintains the bidirectional invariant `device.patient_id ↔ patient.assigned device`.

**Study lifecycle**: ingestion is the only creator (`IN_PROGRESS`). It ends two ways. Explicitly — `POST /studies/{id}/complete|cancel` → `studies_service._transition`, which locks the row `FOR UPDATE` (serialising against an in-flight batch), stamps `ended_at`/`duration_ms`, and re-syncs `patient.study_status`; a second close answers `409 STUDY_NOT_OPEN` rather than a silent 200. Implicitly — unassigning, reassigning or retiring the Holter, and deleting the patient, all call `studies_service.close_open_studies_for_device` / `close_open_studies_for_patient` **before** the `patient_id` is dropped, because afterwards there is no way to tell whose study it was. Moving the vest closes as `COMPLETED` (that is how a Holter normally ends); deleting the patient cancels.

## Data Model Graph

Models in `back/app/db/models/`, base/mixins in `back/app/db/base.py`. All use SQLAlchemy 2.0 `Mapped[...]` typing. UUID PKs + `TimestampMixin` (`id`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `deleted_at` — soft-delete field on all models).

Three-table auth/profile design: `user` (auth identity) ←1:1→ `doctor` (profile for medico users); `doctor` ←1:N→ `patient`; `patient` has an optional `user_id` FK for a future patient mobile app.

## External Integrations Graph

- **Auth0** — identity provider, backend-mediated (ROPG + Management API). Client: `back/app/core/auth0_client.py`. Config: `auth0_*` settings.
- **PostgreSQL** — primary DB. Async engine in `back/app/db/session.py` uses timeouts and `NullPool` in preview/production. Local via root docker-compose (`postgres:16.10-alpine`).
- **S3** — ECG binary blob storage (pre-signed URLs). Client built in `studies_service._get_s3_client()` (boto3, s3v4). Local via docker-compose (`s3` service, RustFS). Config: `s3_*`/`aws_*` settings.
- **Local ECG annotation showcase** — `python -m app.scripts.seed_ecg_showcase` creates/replaces only `SHOWCASE-ECG-ALERTS` and `showcases/ecg-alerts/` in development/test. It writes one deterministic 10-minute study, raw + pyramid objects, and six quality/clinical/patient events across all severities; default owner is `dev@tesis.com`, which must be an active doctor.
- **Vest placement / anomaly test bench** — the vest simulator (`/__sim/vest`, admin-only) drives both patient pushes by hand while `app/ml/*` stays a stub: `VestTestPanel` toggles placement through the device-authenticated `POST /ingest/device-status` and fires `POST /studies/{id}/simulate-anomaly` with the portal session. The mobile side reads placement from `GET /mobile/device` (`vestPlacement`), not from a time window over alerts.
- **Local patient-app alert seed** — `python -m app.scripts.seed_patient_alerts [--dni 44554402]` writes five unanswered `alert` rows (no `ecg_event` behind them) for one patient in development/test, so the mobile app's bell badge, Home pending cards and Notifications list can be reviewed without processing a study. Idempotent: it recognises its own rows by their fixed messages and drops them — plus any `patient_report` answering them — before rewriting.
- **Local ML e2e** — `cd back && uv run python -m tools.e2e_ml` recorre el camino de producción sin mocks: ECG sintético con escenario (`--ectopic-every`, `--tachy-at`, `--pause-at`, `--lead-off-at`) → tramas Rice con el codec del firmware → POSTs de hasta 48 tramas a `/ingest/ecg-frames` con las cabeceras del puente ESP32-C3, go-back-N y espera de `Retry-After` en 503 → `POST /studies/{id}/complete` con la sesión del médico → espera a que `ml_analyzed_samples` y `beats_analyzed_samples` alcancen `samples_count` → `GET /findings` y `/holter-metrics`. `--dry-run` arma los lotes sin red ni base; necesita base, MinIO y API levantados. Las tramas salen con el bit de dato simulado (el estudio queda `is_simulated`, el informe avisa `SIMULATED_STUDY`) y deja médico, paciente, equipo y estudio `e2e-…@holter.test` en la base. Sale con 1 si la ingesta se corta, un lote queda `FAILED` o el análisis no termina.
- **Engine evaluation tools** (`back/tools/`, no corren en CI): `tools/physionet/` mide el motor contra MIT-BIH, NSTDB y QTDB (gate de calidad, morfología, pausas —`--pauses`, con `--wander` y `--firmware-peaks`—, intervalos; README en el directorio) y `tools/physionet/detectors.py` + `tools/vest/detectors.py` son el benchmark de los dos detectores de R (`nk` del motor contra `pt` de las métricas Holter: Se/PPV en MIT-BIH, NSTDB y capturas del chaleco; método y números en `tools/physionet/DETECTORS.md`); `tools/vest/evaluate.py` corre el motor sobre las capturas reales de `../Holter-ECG-System/capturas/` con expectativas y guardas de regresión, incluidas las de ritmo.
- **E2E test user** — `python -m app.scripts.seed_e2e_user --email <e> --auth0-id <sub> [--role medico|admin]` upserts the active `user` row (plus a `doctor` profile for `medico`) bound to an existing Auth0 account, so the Playwright login succeeds; it never calls Auth0, is idempotent, refuses empty values and runs only in development/test. Follow with `seed_demo --doctor-email <e>` to give that doctor data.
- **The Holter device (firmware)** — producer of `ecg_batch` rows + S3 objects through `POST /ingest/ecg-frames`, authenticated with `device.api_key_hash`. The frame format is normative and lives in the sibling repo `../Holter-ECG-System` (`INTEGRACION.md` §3-4). Admins mint credentials with `POST /devices/{id}/api-key`.

## Unknowns / Inferred Edges

- `back/docs/backend/04-autenticacion.md` states "sin autenticación implementada" — **confirmed stale** (auth is fully implemented via Auth0 ROPG + JWT httpOnly cookie). Treat all backend prose docs as potentially stale; source is authoritative.
- `modules/admin` and `modules/ecg_batches` are still source placeholders, intentionally not registered publicly — ingestion lives in `modules/ingest`, because the contract is "receive frames", not "CRUD batches". `modules/alerts` **is now implemented and registered**.
- `PatientStudyStatus.PAUSED` has no writer anywhere: nothing in the domain pauses a study. It is kept in the enum for existing rows but removed from the patients filter UI.
- Beats are detected twice: Pan-Tompkins for the Holter metrics and NeuroKit inside the detection engine. The numbers can differ (NN editing, pause threshold 2.0 s vs 2.5 s); unifying them is pending. Neither pass classifies beats as S/V. El benchmark que mide los dos detectores (`python -m tools.physionet.evaluate --detectors`) está en `back/tools/physionet/DETECTORS.md`.
- `patient.user_id` is no longer just planted: every patient created since the mobile app exists gets an Auth0 account with role `paciente` linked through it. It stays nullable for the patients loaded before that.
- The "anomaly detected" push fires off any HIGH/CRITICAL `alert` created while processing: the rule-based events of `ingest/processing.derive_events` (in practice, the vest's physical symptom button), the detection engine's findings (`ml_pass.pushable`, sent after commit), or the manual `POST /studies/{id}/simulate-anomaly`.
- `patient.study_status` / `patient.last_data_received_at` are a **maintained cache**, not derived state: `ingest_service.ingest_frames` writes them on every accepted batch, and `studies_service` rewrites them on every close. The dashboard counts patients off these columns, so any new path that opens or closes a study must keep them in sync.
- `patient.assigned device` field referenced in the Holter assignment flow is maintained via service-layer logic, not a DB-level column — the actual FK is `device.patient_id` **(inferred naming inconsistency, verify in patients_service.py before editing)**.
